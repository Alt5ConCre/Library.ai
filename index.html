THE SEAR

Four minutes of
total chaos.

0:00

The 90g ball hits 230°C steel and gets pressed flat. Hard. The crust starts here — Maillard, not steam.

1:30

Edges go lacy and rust-brown. We scrape, flip once, and drop the cheddar slab straight on the meat.

3:00

Buns are toasted on the same griddle in beef fat. No toaster. It tastes different and now you know why.

4:00

Stacked in the hand, wrapped, window. From griddle to wrap in under four minutes, every single time.

If the cheese isn't still moving when it reaches you, remade is free. We've remade eleven burgers this year.
